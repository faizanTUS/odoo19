Secure Email OTP Verification | Email OTP Security | User Verification via Email OTP enhances Odoo's user authentication by providing a secure email-based One-Time Password (OTP) verification system. The module strengthens account security by verifying user identities during critical operations such as user registration, login, and other verification workflows, ensuring that only authorized users can access the system.

In addition to OTP verification, the module provides administrators with a centralized view of all OTP verification activities. Admins can monitor OTP requests, view verification statuses for each email address, and manage user access when required. This improves security, simplifies verification tracking, and gives organizations greater control over user authentication processes.

Configure OTP Expiry Settings

After installing the TUS Email OTP module, navigate to Settings > General Settings and configure the OTP Expiry Time. By default, the OTP validity period is set to 2 minutes, ensuring secure and time-limited verification for users.


Grant Access to OTP Verification Records

Navigate to Settings > Users & Companies > Users and select the users who should have access to the OTP Verification menu. Granting this permission allows them to view and manage the OTP verification history for authorized users, as shown in the image below.


Access the OTP Verification Menu

After granting the required access rights, navigate to Settings > Users & Companies. The OTP Verification menu will be available, allowing authorized users to view and track OTP verification records and their statuses, as shown in the image below.


Configure the User Email Address

Navigate to Settings > Users & Companies > Users, then create a new user or open an existing one. Configure the user's Email Address field with a valid email address to enable email-based OTP verification, as shown in the image below.


Enable Email-Based Two-Factor Authentication

Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) using Email for the user. Once enabled, users will be required to verify their identity by entering a One-Time Password (OTP) sent to their registered email address, providing an additional layer of account security.


Verify Email OTP to Enable Two-Factor Authentication

When you enable Email-Based Two-Factor Authentication (2FA), a confirmation dialog will first prompt you to enter your account password. After successful password verification, an OTP Verification window will appear. A One-Time Password (OTP) will be sent to your registered email address. Enter the received OTP to complete the verification process and successfully enable Email 2FA.


Email Two-Factor Authentication Enabled Successfully

After entering the correct One-Time Password (OTP) and completing the verification, a success message will be displayed confirming that Email-Based Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) has been enabled successfully for the user account.


Log In Using Email OTP

On the login page, select the Login with OTP option to authenticate using a One-Time Password (OTP). An OTP will be sent to your registered email address, which you must enter to securely access your account.


Request an Email OTP for Login

Enter your registered Email Address on the login page and click Send OTP. A One-Time Password (OTP) will be sent to your registered email address. Enter the received OTP to securely complete the login process.


Receive and Enter the Email OTP

After clicking the Send OTP button, a One-Time Password (OTP) will be sent to your registered email address. Enter the received OTP to verify your identity and successfully log in to the system.


Enter the OTP and Complete Login

On the OTP verification screen, enter the One-Time Password (OTP) received in your registered email. A countdown timer displays the remaining validity period of the OTP, ensuring it is entered before it expires. Once the correct OTP is entered within the allotted time, the login process is completed successfully.


Handle Expired or Invalid OTPs

If you enter an expired or incorrect OTP, the system will display an error message and deny access. To continue, click the Resend OTP button to receive a new One-Time Password (OTP) on your registered email address, then enter the new OTP to complete the login process.


Resend a New OTP

After clicking the Resend OTP button, a confirmation message will be displayed, and a new One-Time Password (OTP) will be sent to your registered email address. Use the newly received OTP to complete the login process successfully.


Verify Your Email During Sign-Up

On the Sign-Up page, enter your registration details and click Send OTP. A One-Time Password (OTP) will be sent to your registered email address. Enter the received OTP to verify your email and successfully complete the sign-up process.


Enter the OTP to Complete Sign-Up

Enter the One-Time Password (OTP) received in your registered email address into the OTP field. A countdown timer displays the remaining validity period of the OTP, so be sure to enter it before it expires to successfully complete the sign-up verification process.


Complete the Sign-Up Successfully

After entering the correct One-Time Password (OTP), your email will be successfully verified, and your account registration will be completed. You can then access the system using your newly created account.


Prevent Duplicate Email Registration

If you attempt to sign up using an email address that is already registered, the system will display a message indicating that the email address is already in use. To maintain unique user accounts, registration with the same email address is not permitted.


View OTP Verification History

The OTP Verification menu provides a complete history of login and sign-up OTP verification activities. It displays details such as the email address, generated OTP, and verification status, enabling administrators to monitor and track all OTP-related activities within the system.

What is the purpose of this module?

This module provides a secure method of authenticating users during signup and login processes using time-sensitive One-Time Passwords (OTPs) sent to their email addresses. It ensures that only authorized users can access the system.

Can the system be used for both login and signup processes?

Yes, the system supports OTP verification for both signup (user registration) and login (user authentication). It provides an additional layer of security to protect user accounts.

How long is the OTP valid?

The OTP validity duration can be customized. The default expiration time is 2 minutes, but this can be adjusted based on your needs.

What happens if the user enters the wrong OTP?

If the user enters an incorrect OTP, they will be notified, and they can request a new OTP.

Can the OTP be used multiple times?

No, the OTP is a one-time use only code. Once it’s used or expired, it can no longer be used to authenticate the user.

How do I track OTP activities?

The system record each OTP request and verification attempt, allowing administrators to track user activities and identify any issues.

Can I change the expiration time of the OTPs?

Yes, OTP expiration time can be configured in the system settings. Typically, OTPs expire after 5-15 minutes to maintain security.
