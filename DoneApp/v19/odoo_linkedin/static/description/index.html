LinkedIn Integration | Social Post, Image Post & Article Publishing | Content Publishing & HR Recruitment

LinkedIn Integration connects Odoo with LinkedIn, enabling businesses to publish professional content directly from Odoo without switching between applications. Users can create and share company updates, articles, and image posts while managing their LinkedIn accounts from a centralized interface.
The module supports multiple post types, including text posts, article sharing with external URLs, and image posts with attachments. Users can authenticate their LinkedIn accounts securely, select one or multiple LinkedIn users for publishing, and automate posts based on scheduled dates.
To simplify content management, the module records LinkedIn share IDs and maintains publishing information within Odoo. Businesses can organize marketing campaigns, company announcements, blogs, and promotional content while keeping social media activities aligned with their internal workflows.

Fully integrated with Odoo, the module provides a practical solution for LinkedIn content publishing, helping organizations improve brand visibility, simplify social media management, and maintain a consistent online presence.

Share Job Article Post On Member Account and Company Account

Our next feature is the ability to share article type posts on linkedIn. You can use this functionality to share Job articles from Odoo to LinkedIn.

To share this article, please follow the steps below,

Select Post Type as Share Article.

Enter Your Job URL.

Enter Your Job Image URL.

Enter Your Job Title

Share on Member account→ Click on Share Article(tab) to share in a member account.


Share Job Article Post in LinkedIn

When you open linkedIn, you will see an article shared by the Company account from Odoo.


Share Multiple Image Post On Member Account and company account

Our third option, Share image post, allows you to share multiple picture posts from Odoo to LinkedIn.

Make sure you configure the following Valid Details:

Choose Post Type as Share Image post.

Fill your text in the Job Description Field.

The image should be in JPG, PNG, or GIF format.

Attach Your Image In Attachment (In log note)

Click On “Share image” to share in member accounts
