1. What does the AI-Powered Email Generator do?

It uses AI to automatically create professional and personalized emails in Odoo, based on selected fields and context.

2. How does this module improve business communication?

It streamlines email creation, reduces manual work, and ensures consistent, accurate, and timely communication with customers.

3. Can I edit the AI-generated emails before sending?

Yes, you can fully review and adjust the generated email content to match your tone or business style.

4. What types of emails can it generate?

It can craft order confirmations, follow-ups, payment reminders, and other business-related emails automatically.

5. Does the module require an internet connection?

Yes, as it relies on AI models for content generation, an active internet connection is needed.
